01The principle

Each part of the ad is made by the tool that is best at it, and nothing else.

AI makes imagery and motion

Nano Banana 2 draws the characters, sets and first frames. Gemini Omni animates them. They never write text: AI lettering comes out wrong.

Code makes everything exact

Captions, headlines, prices, the map of Tunisia, the phone, the calendar and the logo are React code in Remotion, pixel-checked, right to left where needed.

The edit makes it human

27 short shots, each cut on a spoken word and snapped to the beat, with a designed sound on every action. That's what stops it from feeling AI-generated.

03The system

The voice and the music build a single timing file. Every picture and every sound is placed from it.

8voice lines, 3 takes each. The pick is the take whose transcript matches the script, with clear French words.
27shots in 50 s, about 1.9 s each, every cut placed 60 ms before its word and snapped to the beat.
16AI shots from Gemini Omni, each from its own 2K first frame, at most 2 takes, 720p.
11code shots where exactness matters: the freeze-frame tag, map, call, stopwatch, checklist, calendar, stamp and CTA.
128BPM music bed from Lyria, looped on an exact 64-beat grid, ducked under the voice and cut to silence before the stamp.
0words written by an AI model on screen. All text is code.

08Text never comes from the AI

Every word sits in a zone that the platform's own buttons and captions never cover.

  • Captions follow the voice word by word: Lalezar with a thick black outline, the active word in yellow, never glued across a pause.
  • No word appears twice. When a headline or bubble already shows a word, its caption is hidden.
  • Right to left is measured, not eyeballed. French words inside Arabic lines are isolated so they keep their order.
  • Numbers are digits on screen (300 DT, 4:12, 0 DT), and spelled out in words for the voice.
  • The first frame is the thumbnail: something is lit and readable at frame 0.

09Sound

Half of the feel is sound: a clear Derja voice, a beat the cuts lock to, and a designed sound on every action.

Voice

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS, voice Sadachbia, directed as “Karim”, a young business owner from Tunis. Takes are checked by forced alignment and a second model's transcript.

Music

An instrumental from Lyria 002, beat-tracked at 127.8 BPM and looped on an exact 64-beat grid, so a cut never lands off the beat.

Sound effects

All synthesized in code, so they're fully owned: siren, CCTV glitch, lock-on beeps, cash rustle, keypad, gate clank, shutter, drill, pops, stamp, impacts.

Mix

Music ducks under the voice, goes silent before the stamp, then everything is compressed and mastered to −14 LUFS with a peak limiter.
