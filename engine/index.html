How the engine switches styles

Nothing is hand-edited per style: a style is a data file, and one renderer draws every ad.

A

Content

The approved Derja script, the voice (Gemini TTS, checked word by word), and the timing spine: every cut placed on a spoken word and snapped to the music's beat. Styles A and C share the approved voice; B brings its own script and 5 voices.

B

Style bible

One file per style: the look (prompt block), the cast (casting sheets), the music brief, the design tokens (grade, grain, caption colours) and, for every shot, the first-frame prompt and the Omni motion.

C

Render

Nano Banana frames → Gemini Omni takes (≤ 2) → review (strips, auto-detected cuts) → a plan of backgrounds + overlays per shot → one generic Remotion renderer → master −14 LUFS → QC → this page.
