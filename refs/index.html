LEAD UP · MOTION LAB · ITERATION 0

The references, rebuilt (v0)

Left: the original, played by TikTok's own embed. Right: Claude's quick first rebuild — the same motion (timing, easing, transitions, camera, type), with our own content (InfoLab, Lead Up). Each one was rebuilt from a frame-by-frame study of the original.

v0 means fast and rough: the goal is to check that the style is understood before polishing. Music is our own bed, stretched to each reference's tempo.

r02 · App sizzle: one object carried across every cut

ORIGINAL (TIKTOK EMBED)
CLAUDE · V1 — FULL RESOLUTION, REAL SHUTTER MOTION BLUR, SOUND

What changed in v1

  • Real shutter motion blur like After Effects: every frame is the average of up to 48 sub-frame renders of the whole scene (180° shutter), the count adapting to how far things move; never blurred across a cut. Smooth streaks instead of ghost copies, colours exact
  • After Effects' inertial-bounce expression on every pop (tile, button, stats, badge, props) instead of a generic overshoot curve
  • Moves follow curved paths (tile into the page, '+' into the app tile, badge, logo drop) and use sub-pixel transforms (no 1-px jitter on slow drifts)
  • Turbulent-displace glyph resolve with a big ghost shrinking behind it, like the original's intro
  • Opacity leads position by 1–2 frames on entrances, card faces shade as they flip, the logo lands with squash and a contact shadow
  • A sound on every move (whoosh, whip, keys, pops, ticks, impact, shutter) under our music bed, full 1920×1080
CLAUDE · V0 REBUILD (OUR CONTENT, SAME MOTION)

What Claude did

  • Glyph resolves from a big blur, white tile pops in behind it with overshoot (f0–25)
  • The tile is carried into a store-style page while the ground goes white to black (f38–52); stats pop with a 3-frame stagger
  • Punch-in cut on the beat at f76 (×1.3 between two frames), then a glide
  • A white blob grows out of the button, wipes the page and contracts into the prompt bar's mic dot (f96–112)
  • The '+' button morphs into the app tile while the background hard-cuts to the home screen (f154–166); the mic dot becomes the badge
  • Glyph sucked into the tile with motion blur, tile scaled up into the next ground (f186–210)
  • Three typed chips flip in 3D and stack (f205–278), one white flash frame (f279), logo drops with motion blur and the wordmark slides in

  • Music is our bed time-stretched, no SFX yet
