01What the research says

Ten findings that change how we build ads. Each points to the report it comes from, or to our own test.

1

Native realism beats cinematic polish for leads

Phone-look and documentary creative gets 25-40 % more engagement and lower cost per lead for solar; bill-shock hooks with a real bill beat abstract savings 3-5×. Cinematic is for brand films and retargeting.

01 · 02
2

Omni can make a Tunisian speak Derja on camera

Tested today on our project: Omni said two different Derja lines (one with French words) with matching lip-sync, word for word. Google's docs still say 'not evaluated' for Arabic, so a blind test with Tunisian listeners comes before any client ad.

tested
3

The flatter the style, the more it is code

AI video holds lit, material styles (photo, clay, 3D, painterly). It breaks flat vector, isometric, pixel and line art, and anything with text. Those are built as layers and animated in Remotion; AI does 1-3 hero moments.

03 · tested
4

Lock the style in the still, not in the video prompt

Nano Banana Pro takes up to 3 style references, and our project has it (tested). Make one master frame per style with Pro, then every video prompt only describes motion.

03 · 05 · tested
5

One video model: Omni, with rules per shot

Engine decision: Gemini Omni is the only video model. Faces: close, stable framing and short takes (lip-sync is best under ~7 s); objects and physics: its strength; wide shots: zoom in the edit; exact things are always code.

decision · 01 · 05
6

Style is Meta's cheapest creative diversity

Meta's Andromeda delivery rewards genuinely different visuals, stories and personas. Structure each campaign as concept = angle × style, with hooks and CTAs as variants inside.

05
7

AI judges compare well and score badly

Pairwise comparison agrees with humans far more than 1-10 scores (0.86 vs 0.36). Gates are code and yes/no checklists; takes are picked side by side; the final bar is 'win or tie against the style's best ad'.

05
8

Tunisian means ordinary, not postcard

Flat roofs with water tanks and dishes, tiled salons, cafés, louages, real regional towns. Never Moroccan zellige, Gulf robes, Egyptian lanterns or Santorini domes; Sidi Bou Said at most once.

04
9

Trust beats hype in a scam-aware market

Real faces, the city name, a +216 number, WhatsApp, honest DT numbers. No fake testimonials (platforms and Law 92-117); always switch on the AI disclosure; no realistic children.

02 · 04
10

The 2027 story is the battery

Summer 2026 power cuts, and STEG authorising home batteries on 24 July 2026, make 'battery + panels' the lead solar hook. Campaigns should start March-May 2027; Ramadan wants humour, nostalgia and family.

Can the AI speak Tunisian Derja on camera?

Same first frame (Nano Banana Pro), same line for both models. Turn the sound on. The transcripts under each video were made by Gemini 2.5 Pro from the audio.

وليدي، ركّبت برشا caméras هالعام… أما الـ clients؟ Lead Up هوما إلّي جابوهملي.

Watch out: when no line is given, video models invent speech (an early test spoke English; the café test mumbled). Rule: every speaking shot carries its exact Derja line, or is muted and dubbed with our voice.

Verdict: Omni delivers the words and the mouth follows them, twice in a row. Before any client ad, three Tunisians rate the accent blind; every AI person is disclosed and never presented as a real customer.

11The engine that switches styles

The same approved script can be rendered in any style. What changes is a style bible (data), not the pipeline.

A

Content — same for every style

Script → Derja voice → word timings → beats → ~30 shot intents (what each shot must show, not how it looks).

B

Style library — one bible per style

Look, palette, lens, texture, motion rules, music, SFX, caption style, do/don't, a master frame (Nano Banana Pro), approved examples, the best finished ad, QC thresholds.

C

Render — ad × style

The router picks frames (NB Pro / NB2), motion (Omni / parallax / code), judges pick takes, Remotion applies the style theme, then master, QC and publish.

12What "10/10" means, automatically

Three layers, in order. An ad ships only when all three pass, then the owner watches it on a phone.

1 · Hard gates (code)

  • Frame 0 lit and readable; something changes by 0.5 s
  • Every on-screen string equals the approved text; right-to-left measured
  • Text size, time on screen and contrast; clear of the Reels overlay zones
  • No glyphs in any AI shot; no inserted cuts; no frozen, black or flickering frames
  • Cuts on beats or words; no clip ends on a settling image
  • −14 LUFS, ≤ −1.5 dBTP; voice transcript ≤ 5 % off the script
  • yuv420p, TV range, BT.709; average luma 90-190
  • Logo and CTA present; claims match the brief; AI disclosure recorded

2 · Yes/no checklist (Gemini judge)

  • Hands, faces and objects stay intact
  • Physics plausible; on-style
  • The shot's intent happens in the kept part
  • No AI tells: glow, bokeh balls, waxy skin
  • Message clear with sound off
  • Hook creates a question in 2 s
  • Every "no" must give a timestamp

3 · Side by side

  • Takes are picked in pairs, both orders
  • The new ad must win or tie against the style's best ad
  • A new winner becomes the bar (a ratchet)
  • Judge ≈ 5 % of the generation cost

13Build order

  1. Split today's pipeline into the three layers (content · style bible · render) and add the code gates.
  2. Style bibles for 3 styles: 3D clay (done), iPhone-native documentary (photoreal), and one code-heavy style (isometric or kinetic). Re-render the solar script in all three to prove the switch.
  3. Omni rules per shot type: speaking shots with their Derja line, faces short and stable, orbits in code.
  4. Derja talking-head blind test: Omni speaking vs our TTS voice, rated by 3 Tunisians.
  5. The judge: checklist + pairwise, calibrated on past takes; then variants per campaign as angle × style.
